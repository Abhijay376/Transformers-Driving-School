VicRoads Drive Test Pakenham

Everything you need to know about the Pakenham VicRoads test centre: test centre details, the two-stage test format, and how Transformers Driving School instructors prepare Pakenham learners for test day.

Reviewed by Sol Pakzad, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor
10+ Years Experience Test-Day Car Options Flexible Instructor Options VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructors

Test Centre Details

LocationVicRoads Pakenham Customer Service Centre, Pakenham VIC 3810 (exact suite/street number changes periodically — confirm on your booking confirmation)
HoursMonday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm (by appointment only)
Public transit accessClose to Pakenham railway station (Pakenham line) and the Pakenham bus interchange, with several local bus routes serving the town centre

Quick facts

  • Minor faults: up to 15 allowed before failing
  • Test runs in two stages: 10 minutes + 20 minutes
  • Pre-drive safety check required before you start driving
  • One of the busier growth-corridor test centres, so book appointments early

Test Format & Rules

Minor vs. Major / Dangerous Faults

  • Minor faults: up to 15 allowed across the whole test
  • 16th minor fault results in an automatic fail
  • Major or dangerous faults result in an immediate fail, regardless of minor fault count

Pre-Drive Safety Checks Checklist

  • Horn
  • Indicators
  • Wipers
  • Brake lights
  • Demisters (front & rear)
  • Boot
  • Ignition in neutral / park before starting

Stage 1: Basic Controls

Duration: 10 minutes

Low-risk manoeuvres such as a 3-point turn or reverse parallel park, usually on the quieter residential streets near the centre. You must pass Stage 1 before progressing to Stage 2.

Stage 2: Busy Traffic

Duration: 20 minutes

Conducted on busier roads around Pakenham: the Princes Highway corridor, roundabouts, lane changes, and complex intersections through the town centre and growth-corridor estates.

Immediate Termination Errors

  • Intervention by the tester
  • Disobeying a direction/sign
  • Collision
  • Failing to give way
  • Excessive speed
  • Stopping in a dangerous position
  • Failing to stop
  • Dangerous action

Critical Errors

More than 1 in Stage 1, or more than 2 overall, ends the test.

  • Too slow
  • Fail to look
  • Fail to signal
  • Blocking a pedestrian crossing
  • Mounting the kerb
  • Stalling
  • Incomplete stop
  • Illegal action

Local Pakenham Test Roads & Fail Points

General guidance based on the type of roads used around the Pakenham test centre. Note: exact current test routes vary by tester and can change over time, so treat this as a guide to the kinds of conditions to practise, not a guaranteed route.

1

Princes Highway corridor 80 km/h

High-speed merging and lane discipline is a common focus, since Pakenham’s main highway corridor runs directly through the area.

2

Pakenham town centre streets 50 km/h

Busy town-centre streets with pedestrian crossings and frequent give-way situations near shops and the train station.

3

Local roundabouts 50 / 60 km/h

Roundabout entry and exit lane discipline is a common focus area in Stage 2, given the number of roundabouts across the growth-corridor estates.

4

Residential estate streets 40 / 50 km/h

Quieter residential streets near the centre are commonly used for Stage 1 low-speed manoeuvres such as 3-point turns and reverse parallel parks.

Vehicle Requirements

Using your own vehicle

  • Roadworthy and currently registered
  • Working horn, indicators, brake lights, headlights, wipers
  • Functioning front and rear demisters
  • Valid insurance and correctly displayed L-plates

Using an TDS instructor vehicle

  • Already meets all VicRoads compliance requirements
  • Dual-control for added safety
  • Included in the Express Test Package and Ultimate Test Pass Pack
