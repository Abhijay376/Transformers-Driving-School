Victoria’s 3-Tier Overseas Licence System

Every country falls into one of three tiers, which determines what, if anything, you need to do to convert your licence in Victoria.

TierExample countriesAge / experience requirementWhat’s required
1. Recognised countryUK, Ireland, Canada, Germany, Japan, South KoreaNo specific requirementDirect exchange, no test in most cases
2. Experienced Driver RecognitionIndia, USA, South Africa, Philippines, MalaysiaUnder 25 usually needs knowledge + practical test; 25+ with 3+ years’ experience may qualify for a more direct pathwayKnowledge test, and often a practical drive test, depending on age/experience
3. Other countryAll countries not listed under tiers 1 or 2Applies regardless of age or experienceFull Victorian process: knowledge test, hazard perception test, practical driving test

Step-by-Step Conversion Process

  1. Check your tier

    Use the checker above to confirm whether your issuing country is Recognised, Experienced Driver Recognition, or Other.

  2. Gather your documents

    Valid overseas licence, passport/visa, proof of address, and an official translation if your licence isn’t in English.

  3. Book a knowledge test (if required)

    Tiers 2 and 3 generally require a computer-based knowledge test on Victorian road rules.

  4. Complete a hazard perception test (Tier 3)

    Required as part of the full licensing process for countries not covered by an exchange or recognition agreement.

  5. Take your practical drive test

    Where required, TDS instructors can prepare you specifically for your local VicRoads test centre. See our test centre guide for Pakenham.

  6. Receive your Victorian licence

    Once you pass the required steps, your Victorian driver licence is issued and your overseas licence is typically surrendered.
