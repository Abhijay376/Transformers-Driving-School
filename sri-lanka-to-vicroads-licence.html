Sri Lanka to VicRoads Driving Licence Conversion

How to convert your Sri Lanka driving licence to a Victorian licence: your tier, requirements, and step-by-step process.

Reviewed by Sol Pakzad, VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructor
10+ Years Experience Test-Day Car Options Flexible Instructor Options VicRoads ADI Accredited Instructors

If you’re moving to Victoria with a Sri Lanka driving licence and want to convert overseas licence Victoria requirements into a plain checklist, you’re in the right place. This guide covers exactly what a Sri Lanka licence holder needs to do to convert their licence to a Victorian one, and it forms part of our broader guide to convert international licence Melbourne newcomers can follow for any country.

Your Tier: Full Victorian Licensing Process

The full Victorian process applies: a knowledge test, a hazard perception test, and a practical driving test.

Sri Lankan driving licences are not currently on Victoria’s Recognised or Experienced Driver Recognition lists, so the full Victorian licensing process applies.

Age / Experience Requirement

Applies regardless of age or driving experience.

Step-by-Step: Sri Lanka Licence to Victorian Licence

  1. Confirm your tier

    Use our checker to confirm your Sri Lanka licence falls under the Full Victorian Licensing Process tier before starting the process.

  2. Gather your documents

    Valid Sri Lanka licence, passport/visa, proof of Victorian address, and an official English translation if your licence isn’t in English.

  3. Book any required tests

    The full Victorian process applies: a knowledge test, a hazard perception test, and a practical driving test.

  4. Prepare with a local instructor

    If a practical test is required, TDS instructors can prepare you for your specific VicRoads test centre. See our guide for Pakenham.

  5. Receive your Victorian licence

    Once you’ve completed the required steps, your Victorian driver licence is issued and your Sri Lanka licence is typically surrendered.
